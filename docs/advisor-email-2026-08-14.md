Since the last memo:

- I closed the last open measurement question. Price formation is now checked

  fifteen-minute intervals.

- I ran the Kalshi re-harvest, so the season stays priced before the

- There is a new finding on the one real anomaly in the project. Kalshi
  misprices small margins of victory in baseball. I collected a third market
  layer, total runs scored, and tested it on the same 1,244 games: the implied
  distributions of total runs are correct while the implied distributions of
  the margin are not. So the market is not modeling baseball badly. It is
  mishandling the walk-off rule that ends the game the moment the home team
  goes ahead.

- I added a section connecting the results to Betterment's new survey, which
  found that about a quarter of Gen Z investors treat sports betting as part
  of a long-term financial plan. The project's numbers speak to that directly,
  and the answer is not the obvious one: these markets are priced about as well
  as the professional sportsbooks, and that still does not make them a
  sensible way to hold money.

The evidence phase is complete. There are 57 analyses, all passing the data
