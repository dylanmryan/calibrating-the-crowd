## Abstract (write last)
One sentence each: question → design (three institutionally distinct sources,
same 5,000+ games) → headline (formal equivalence) → distributional/horizon
depth → institutional synthesis (surface vs aggregate).

## 1. Introduction
- Hook: two mechanisms priced the same ~5,000 games — a CFTC-regulated exchange,
  an offshore crypto CLOB, and (benchmark) Vegas consensus. Did the crowd match
  the professionals?
- Contribution list: (i) first three-way same-game calibration with a books
  benchmark; (ii) formal equivalence (TOST), not just "no significant difference";
  (iii) distributional layer (ladders/PIT); (iv) horizon-resolved: *when* the
  equivalence forms; (v) institutional cost synthesis; (vi) methods lesson
  (side-assignment audit).

## 2. Literature
- Page & Clemen (2013): calibration improves toward event date — we replicate
  comparatively (both exchanges sharpen in lockstep; `horizon_cross`).
- Snowberg & Wolfers (2010): favorite–longshot as misperception — we find *no*
  FLB in any source (slopes ≈1), ladder longshots if anything underpriced.
- Woodland & Woodland (1994): MLB moneylines efficient-within-costs, reverse
  FLB — baseline for our run-line context note.
- Clinton & Huang (2026): cross-platform equivalence question — we add the
  books benchmark their design lacked.
- Bürgi, Deng & Whelan (2026) "Makers and Takers": platform-wide Kalshi FLB,
  −20% average ROI, makers > takers (all categories, pre-sports sample). Our
  sports replication (§5.8) shows those pathologies vanish in sports — the
  benchmark-discipline mechanism argument.
- Practitioner run-line literature (home −1.5 ≈45% cover, walk-off compression)
  — corroborates the Layer-2 cell.
- Institutional context: CFTC self-certification, Polymarket fee introduction
  (natural experiment, `fee_experiment`).

## 4. Methods

### 5.3 When the equivalence forms (`horizon*`, `close_efficiency`)
Books lead at T−24h (p=.029) → lockstep sharpening, indistinguishable at all 7

### 5.4 Microstructure: how prices form (`lead_lag`, `informed`)
No venue leads at 15-min; big repricings simultaneous (event study); markout
flat across trade size → maker-driven discovery; book stickiness ≠ momentum
(small-sample artifact, retired honestly).

## 6. Institutional synthesis
"Sportsbook-like at the retail-taker surface (taker all-in ≈ vig; biases inside
cost bands), forecasting instrument in aggregate (equivalent, equally sharp,
coherent, maker-driven discovery)." ⚖ framing check with advisor.

## 7. Limitations
Sports-only scope; consensus book line (not one book's risk book); Kalshi
60-day price decay (harvest protocol); trade-recon staleness; constant-sample
composition; small-league power; lead–lag resolution (15-min); WC n=9;
multiple-testing policy pre-registered only informally.

## 8. Reproducibility
Free-API re-derivability (Kalshi/Poly); book data = code + instructions;
`src.make_results` one-command regeneration; secrets rotation before public.
